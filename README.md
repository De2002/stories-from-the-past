# Stories From the Past

Build LeBeHo.

Instagram for old stuff.

A global social network where the past gets a place in everyone's feed. This is an images only social platform.  People can post images of old things.  People don't just upload objects; they explain their history, significance or personal memories.

Social networking comes first. Following people, discovering posts, commenting, liking and collecting should feel natural, just like Instagram. The vision: Someone opens PikPuk intending to spend two minutes looking at old photographs and ends up spending thirty minutes discovering vintage objects, forgotten places, old technology and stories from around the world. Photograph posting with backstories.

Individual post pages.

User profiles and following.

Likes, comments and collections. Product type: Visual social network

Content: Old things, vintage items and historical photographs.

Core experience: Photograph-first feeds with stories and backstories.

Audience: Collectors, nostalgia lovers, history enthusiasts, vintage enthusiasts and curious explorers.

Geography: Worldwide.The idea is simple: people share pictures of old things, discover things from other people's pasts, and learn the stories behind what they're seeing. Use lovable cloud. Also seed a few accurate stuff.
